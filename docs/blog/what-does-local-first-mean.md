We want to make AppWeaver a place for programmers to develop their applications and reach out to users through our "platform". 

AppWeaver itself is not a decentralized protocol because I think there is no need. Why do we need decentralization in the first place? Because we don't want to lock ourself into a tech company and become their slaves because moving out is losing your network effect you built throughout the years by investing your time and energy, and your data is often not easy to migrate.

But for AppWeaver it's already [local-first](what-does-local-first-mean), meaning your data is yours and the software you're running is fully open-source. You can install any plugin/app into your workspace, or even create a new one. You can even change core the code and restart the application, everything is totally in your control.

With this philosophy, I decided not to create a protocol, but instead a place -a platform- that provides infrastructure for other applications. We have a cashu wallet, nostr identification system, AI backends you can choose from, and a plugin manager. This plugin manager runs on top of a custom nostr protocol. It's a marketplace for AppWeaver specific applications. Anyone can publish a new plugin and AppWeaver users would see them as published from their instances.
